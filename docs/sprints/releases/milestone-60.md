# Vision Sprint 61

Works when you show it: when an artist or a partner first looks at the preview, the features they are shown work end to end, with no step that says "not built yet".

- **Remix approval:** a remix creator asks, and the artist previews, then approves or declines one remix version. Approval unlocks publish and export for that version only, a new version needs a new approval, and a decline keeps the remix a private draft and tells the creator.
- **Shows interest in artist analytics:** interest totals and counts by city, with no city shown below the minimum group.
- **Stem previews counted:** each stem preview writes one record, and the weekly count shows on the preview measures page.
- **Next step for remix approvals (stretch, first slice):** artist analytics lists a card while remix creators wait for the artist's answer, linking to the approval page.

Application milestone: [#60](https://github.com/akoita/resonate/milestone/60). All three items and the first slice of the stretch item shipped. This is a changelog for the milestone, not a software version or deployment event.
